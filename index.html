About Counsel & Firm

Committed to Legal Excellence, Procedural Rigor & Client Trust

I am Advocate Abhishek Kumar Pandey, actively practicing before the prestigious High Court of Judicature at Allahabad. As the founder of Abhishek Pandey & Associates, my legal practice is founded on deep statutory analysis, transparent litigation strategy, and zealous advocacy.

Our chambers handle a wide spectrum of litigations with a strong focus on Constitutional Writs, Bail applications, High Court appeals, Criminal Defense, Family disputes, and Complex Property & Revenue matters. We understand the high stakes involved in legal battles and prioritize diligent drafting, prompt hearings, and direct communication.

Legal Practice Spectrum

Core Areas of Litigation & Advisory

Comprehensive litigation management from trial courts to the High Court of Allahabad.

High Court Litigation

Representation in original and appellate proceedings before the Allahabad High Court, revision petitions, and special leave guidance.

Writ Petitions

Constitutional remedies under Article 226 including Writs of Mandamus, Certiorari, Habeas Corpus, and Prohibition against administrative inaction.

Criminal Defense & Bail

Anticipatory bail, regular bail, Section 482 CrPC petitions for quashing FIRs/charge-sheets, and criminal trial defense.

Civil & Commercial Disputes

Injunction suits, recovery proceedings, contract breaches, execution petitions, and First/Second Appeals before the High Court.

Property & Revenue Matters

Land acquisition challenges, UP Revenue Code disputes, partition suits, title declarations, mutation, and mutation appeals.

Family & Matrimonial

Matrimonial dispute resolution, domestic violence petitions, maintenance (125 CrPC / BNSS), custody, and succession matters.
